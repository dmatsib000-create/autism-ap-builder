2. APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS (ABA) THERAPY
ABA therapy is recommended as the primary evidence-based behavioral intervention for her and is medically necessary given her confirmed diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder, Level 2 (Social Communication) / Level 1 (Restricted/Repetitive Behaviors), with the following documented functional impairments establishing medical necessity: behavioral rigidity and significant difficulty with transitions; expressive language delays; social skill impairments (social reciprocity); significant adaptive impairment. These impairments substantially limit her ability to participate safely in daily activities, access educational programming, and benefit from less intensive interventions without individualized ABA support. ABA is explicitly endorsed by the American Academy of Pediatrics¹ and is supported by federal comparative effectiveness review² as effective for improving communication, adaptive behavior, social skills, and quality of life outcomes in individuals with ASD.
Early Intensive Behavioral Intervention (EIBI) - a structured, comprehensive ABA-based program - is indicated for children under age 6. A Cochrane systematic review³ demonstrates robust improvement in language, cognitive, and adaptive behavior outcomes with EIBI. Recommended intensity to be determined by the treating BCBA following individualized skills assessment and per medical necessity criteria. Services should span home, clinic, and natural-environment settings; caregiver coaching is a core component of effective early intervention.
Families who have access to Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Intervention (NDBI) programs (e.g., Early Start Denver Model, Pivotal Response Training, JASPER) should engage or continue with these approaches. NDBI-based ABA (embedding behavioral principles within naturalistic, relationship-focused, and play-based interactions) carries a particularly strong evidence base for children in the infant-toddler-preschool period and represents a preferred style of early intensive intervention when accessible.
