REQUEST FOR IEP TEAM MEETING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I understand that {Student Name} currently has an Individualized Education Program (IEP) in place. I respectfully request that the IEP team convene to review and update the IEP in light of the current evaluation findings documented above.

Florida Rule 6A-6.03028(3)(j)2.c. asks the IEP Team to revise the IEP as appropriate to address information about the student provided by the parents, and I hope the enclosed evaluation is useful in that review. I ask that a meeting be scheduled at the family's earliest convenience to consider the following updates.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED SERVICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I recommend the following updates to the current IEP:

Physical Therapy (PT): School-based PT services are recommended to address motor needs that affect safe navigation of the school environment and access to physical education. Goals: postural control and core strengthening to address low muscle tone affecting endurance, positioning, and sustained classroom participation; safe navigation of the school campus (stairs, hallways, playground equipment).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED ACCOMMODATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I recommend that the IEP team consider the following accommodations, calibrated to evaluation findings and individual educational need. These are clinical recommendations, not legal entitlements; the team's selection should reflect what the evaluation supports.

Core ASD-Related Accommodations:
The following accommodations have broad evidence support across the autism spectrum. Implementation should be calibrated to this student's developmental level and individual presentation.
  • Predictable daily schedule with advance written or visual notice before transitions (schedule format and complexity calibrated to developmental level)
  • Visual supports (format calibrated to developmental level and literacy): pictorial schedule and visual transition cues for younger or non-reading students; written agenda, task checklists, or digital planner for older or verbal students — visual representation of schedules, task steps, and expectations reduces transition anxiety and supports regulation across the autism spectrum

Individually Indicated Accommodations Based on Evaluation Findings:
  • Motor accommodations: keyboarding as alternative to handwriting, extended time on written tasks, adapted physical education referral if indicated
