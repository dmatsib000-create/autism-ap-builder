LETTER OF MEDICAL NECESSITY
Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) Therapy — Autism Spectrum Disorder

Date: [DATE]
Diagnosis: Autism Spectrum Disorder, Level 3 — Requiring Very Substantial Support, with accompanying intellectual disability (DSM-5)
ICD-10-CM: F84.0

To Whom It May Concern / Dear Medical Director:

I write to request authorization for Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy for the patient, who has received a confirmed diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Level 3 — Requiring Very Substantial Support, as defined by the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition (DSM-5; American Psychiatric Association, 2013) enrolled under Medicaid/CHIP. ABA therapy is medically necessary, as documented below.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I. DIAGNOSIS & LEVEL OF SUPPORT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Severe deficits in verbal and nonverbal social communication cause marked functional impairment across all settings. She demonstrates minimal initiation of social interactions and limited response to social overtures from others. Extreme difficulty coping with change and restricted/repetitive behaviors interfere with functioning in all spheres of daily life. Intensive ABA therapy is medically necessary given the very substantial support needs documented, the severity of communication and behavioral impairments, and the significant risk to her safety and development without structured behavioral intervention.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
II. FUNCTIONAL IMPAIRMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
She demonstrates clinically significant functional impairments across the following domains: expressive and/or receptive communication; behavioral regulation and reduction of interfering behaviors; safety awareness and risk management; sensory processing and modulation; adaptive and daily living skills. With respect to communication, she is nonverbal/minimally verbal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
III. TREATMENT NECESSITY & EVIDENCE BASE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ABA therapy, the most extensively studied behavioral intervention for ASD, is explicitly recommended as a primary evidence-based treatment by the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP).¹ Multiple federal agency systematic reviews confirm its efficacy across communication, adaptive behavior, and quality-of-life outcomes.²

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IV. REQUESTED TREATMENT PARAMETERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Requested intensity: 40 hours per week
Priority treatment targets:
  • comm
  • sib
  • aggression
  • elopement

Treatment will be implemented by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) who will oversee program development, conduct ongoing assessment, and adjust treatment based on objective data. Direct therapy will be provided by trained Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) under BCBA supervision, consistent with BACB professional and ethical standards.
