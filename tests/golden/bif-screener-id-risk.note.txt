Brief cognitive screening places cognitive functioning in the borderline range. This does not exclude an intellectual disability: comprehensive testing may place cognitive functioning within the intellectual disability range, and a confirmed borderline score accompanied by impaired adaptive functioning may still support that diagnosis. Comprehensive psychological evaluation recommended to formally characterize cognitive and adaptive profiles.
