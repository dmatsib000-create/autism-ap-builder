I evaluated {Student Name} on {Date of Evaluation} and am writing to provide a formal medical recommendation in support of the family's request for special education services under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).

Specifically, I am requesting a comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation to determine eligibility for special education services. Florida allows 60 calendar days from written parental consent for that evaluation, with the exclusions the rule provides. The findings supporting that request are set out below, together with the services and accommodations I recommend.

They have received a diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), suspected (ICD-10-CM: Z03.89), as documented at the evaluation referenced above. In addition, they carry the following co-occurring diagnoses, each of which contributes to the educational challenges described below: a specific learning disability in written expression (dysgraphia).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EDUCATIONAL IMPACT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
These conditions result in the following challenges that require specialized support in the educational setting:
  • Communication: Significant difficulty with verbal and nonverbal communication, including understanding classroom instructions, expressing needs, and participating in peer conversation and group activities.
  • Motor skills: Motor difficulties affecting handwriting and written output, within the school setting.
  • Adaptive skills: Difficulty with daily living and self-care skills that affect independent functioning within the school setting.
  • Safety awareness: family reports limited awareness of hazards in community settings, which may affect safety during arrival and dismissal, field trips, and other less-structured school settings.
  • Written expression: A specific learning disability in written expression affecting the ability to produce written work at grade level.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION EVALUATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Based on the above diagnosis and identified educational impact, I strongly recommend that {Student Name} receive a comprehensive evaluation for special education eligibility under IDEA, including consideration of the Autism Spectrum Disorder eligibility category (34 C.F.R. §300.8(c)(1); Florida Rule 6A-6.03023) and any additional eligibility categories supported by the evaluation results.

The evaluation should include assessment of: cognitive abilities, academic achievement, adaptive behavior, communication skills, behavioral and social-emotional functioning, and sensory-motor development.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED SERVICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If eligible for special education services, I recommend the IEP include the following:

Speech-Language Pathology (SLP): School-based SLP services are recommended to address the documented communication needs that directly affect classroom access. Recommended goals include: expressive language development: expanding expressive language for classroom participation, with specific targets and communication modality to be determined by the school SLP evaluation.

Occupational Therapy (OT): School-based OT services are recommended to address sensory, fine motor, and/or adaptive needs that affect classroom participation and academic output. Goals: fine motor and graphomotor development: pencil grip, letter formation, and/or keyboarding as a compensatory strategy for written output; adaptive self-care skills relevant to the school day: clothing management such as jackets and fasteners, and related self-care routines.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED ACCOMMODATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I recommend that the IEP team consider the following accommodations, calibrated to evaluation findings and individual educational need. These are clinical recommendations, not legal entitlements; the team's selection should reflect what the evaluation supports.

Core ASD-Related Accommodations:
The following accommodations have broad evidence support across the autism spectrum. Implementation should be calibrated to this student's developmental level and individual presentation.
  • Predictable daily schedule with advance written or visual notice before transitions (schedule format and complexity calibrated to developmental level)
  • Visual supports (format calibrated to developmental level and literacy): pictorial schedule and visual transition cues for younger or non-reading students; written agenda, task checklists, or digital planner for older or verbal students — visual representation of schedules, task steps, and expectations reduces transition anxiety and supports regulation across the autism spectrum

Individually Indicated Accommodations:
  • Motor accommodations: reduced copying and handwriting demands, and extended time on written tasks
  • Safety supervision: planned adult supervision during arrival and dismissal, transitions between areas, and off-campus activities such as field trips, with the level of supervision determined by the school team

Behavioral and Emotional Accommodations:
  • Communication: AAC device access if applicable, visual and written directions in addition to verbal instructions, SLP consultation for classroom communication accommodations

Academic Accommodations:
  • Written expression: speech-to-text, keyboarding alternative to handwriting, extended time, reduced written output requirements
