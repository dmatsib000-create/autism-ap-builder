  Note: PCIT is clinically distinct from the parent training component of ABA. PCIT targets the parent-child relationship through structured in-session coaching (Child-Directed Interaction and Parent-Directed Interaction phases), with a primary goal of improving relational attunement and consistent, contingent limit-setting. ABA parent training targets caregiver implementation of behavioral reinforcement strategies in naturalistic routines to generalize skill acquisition.
