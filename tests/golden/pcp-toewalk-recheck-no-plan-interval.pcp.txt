RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE from Developmental-Behavioral Pediatrics (DBP)

DBP follow-up:
  • No routine DBP visits are planned; you can follow the child with the plan below. This is not a discharge: the child remains in our care and can return as needed. We are glad to see the child and family again for any reason listed under "Re-refer to DBP if". Thank you for caring for this child.
  • How to come back: within 2 years of the last visit, the family or your office can call us directly at (352) 265-8250. No new referral is needed. After 2 years, or if the child was dismissed from our clinic, please send a new referral.

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Ask whether speech-language therapy and physical therapy have started. If not, ask where the family is on each waitlist. The insurance case manager can find in-network providers, and families often have to call a waitlist more than once.
  • PCP (next visit): Check that this referral is scheduled or done: clinical genetics. We placed every referral. Some insurance plans want the referral to come from primary care; if so, please re-send it. You do not need to order genetic testing: clinical genetics orders it and follows up the results.
  • PCP (next well visit, no later than 12 months): Check how far the ankle bends up, with the knee straight and with it bent, and watch the child walk. Refer to physical therapy if the ankle no longer reaches neutral with the knee straight, or if pain, falls, or trouble with daily activities develop.

Re-refer to DBP if:
  • A new developmental, learning, attention, or mood concern needs a diagnostic evaluation.
  • Self-injury, aggression, or unsafe behavior continues despite current supports, or you are considering medication for behavior.
  • School, insurance, disability services, or the move to adult care needs updated diagnostic testing or documentation.

Urgent, or for another service:
  • The child loses language, social, or motor skills they already had: refer to pediatric neurology.
