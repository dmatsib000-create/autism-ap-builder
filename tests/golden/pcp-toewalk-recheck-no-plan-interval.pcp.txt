RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE from Developmental-Behavioral Pediatrics (DBP)

DBP follow-up:
  • No routine DBP follow-up is planned. The child has not been discharged and can return as needed. We are glad to see the child and family again for any reason listed under "Re-refer to DBP if". Thank you for continuing this child's care.
  • To come back: within 2 years of the last visit here, the family or primary care can call our clinic directly at (352) 265-8250; no new referral is needed. After 2 years, or if the child has been dismissed from our clinic, please send a new referral.

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that speech-language therapy and physical therapy have started. If not, ask where the family is on each waitlist and point them to the insurance case manager for in-network providers; waitlists often need repeated calls.
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that this referral is scheduled or completed: clinical genetics. Our clinic placed every referral; some insurance plans need one re-sent from primary care. Clinical genetics orders and follows the genetic testing, so none needs to be ordered in primary care.
  • PCP (next well visit, no later than 12 months): Recheck ankle range (knee straight and bent) and gait. Refer to PT if the ankle no longer reaches neutral with the knee straight, or if pain, falls, or functional impact develops.

Re-refer to DBP if:
  • A new developmental, learning, attention, or mood concern needs diagnostic evaluation.
  • Self-injury, aggression, or unsafe behavior persists despite the current supports, or medication for behavior is being considered.
  • Updated diagnostic testing or documentation is required for school, insurance, disability services, or the move to adult care.

Urgent, or for another service:
  • Language, social, or motor skills the child already had are lost: refer to pediatric neurology.
