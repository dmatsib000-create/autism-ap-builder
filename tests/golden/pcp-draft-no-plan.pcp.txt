RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that ABA, speech-language therapy, occupational therapy, and psychotherapy have started. If not, the family should ask the insurance case manager for in-network providers and keep calling the waitlists.
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that these referrals are scheduled or completed: clinical genetics and child psychiatry. Clinical genetics orders and follows the genetic testing, so none needs to be ordered in primary care.

Primary care can monitor and manage (no routine DBP follow-up needed for these):
  • Mood and safety: Recheck mood and safety in 4 to 6 weeks, screening for suicide risk at that visit with the NIMH ASQ (Ask Suicide-Screening Questions). A positive screen is followed by the brief suicide safety assessment in the ASQ toolkit.

DBP follow-up:
  • {DBP follow-up plan: no routine DBP follow-up, or a return visit and its purpose}

Re-refer to DBP if:
  • A new developmental, learning, attention, or mood concern needs diagnostic evaluation.
  • Self-injury, aggression, or unsafe behavior persists despite the current supports, or medication for behavior is being considered.
  • Updated diagnostic testing or documentation is required for school, insurance, disability services, or the move to adult care.

Refer directly to another service if:
  • Language, social, or motor skills the patient already had are lost: refer to pediatric neurology.
  • Mania, psychosis, or substance use that complicates treatment appears: refer to child psychiatry.
  • Suicidal thoughts or behavior appear: use emergency or crisis care (call or text 988).
