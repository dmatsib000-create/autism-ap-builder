2. APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS (ABA) THERAPY
Early behavioral intervention is recommended given their age and the following documented developmental impairments: tantrums/meltdowns; expressive language delays. ABA-based services are the most extensively studied and supported intervention for young children with ASD or suspected ASD. Recommended intensity to be determined by the treating BCBA following individualized skills assessment; services should span home, clinic, and natural-environment settings, with caregiver coaching embedded throughout and coordination with Early Intervention services.
Families who have access to Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Intervention (NDBI) programs (e.g., Early Start Denver Model, Pivotal Response Training, JASPER) should engage or continue with these approaches. NDBI-based ABA carries a particularly strong evidence base for children in the infant-toddler-preschool period and represents a preferred style of early intervention when accessible.
  • Parent/caregiver training: Caregiver-implemented intervention is a required and evidence-based component of ABA services; specific training content is determined by the treating BCBA and commonly spans the function of target behaviors, reinforcement strategies in natural routines, generalization of clinic and school skills to the home, and crisis response for high-intensity behaviors. Parent training should be documented as a distinct billable service, not subsumed under child direct therapy hours.
  • Least restrictive and assent-based practice: We support programming that uses the least restrictive, most naturalistic procedures appropriate to the child's profile, seeks patient assent as developmentally appropriate, honors and documents assent withdrawal, and relies on positive-reinforcement-based strategies as first-line, with restrictive procedures reserved for documented justification and oversight, consistent with BACB ethical standards.

3. PARENT-CHILD INTERACTION THERAPY (PCIT)
Recommended for: tantrums.
Evidence-based, parent-mediated intervention improving the parent-child relationship while reducing disruptive behaviors. Referral to a PCIT-trained therapist is recommended.
  Note: PCIT is clinically distinct from the parent training component of ABA. PCIT targets the parent-child relationship through structured in-session coaching (Child-Directed Interaction and Parent-Directed Interaction phases), with a primary goal of improving relational attunement and consistent, contingent limit-setting. ABA parent training targets caregiver implementation of behavioral reinforcement strategies in naturalistic routines to generalize skill acquisition. When both are in place, goals and billable sessions should be documented as non-overlapping.

  At-Home Strategies While Waiting for Formal Therapy:

  OPT-In-Early (Online Parent Training in Early Intervention)
  Free, comprehensive, self-guided online program from the University of Connecticut for families of young children with signs of autism or other developmental concerns, including those awaiting a diagnosis. Covers 14 modules: communication, social skills, behavior, daily routines, and more. Grounded in ABA and naturalistic developmental principles. Optional modules for picky eating and toilet training.
  https://parenttraining.chip.uconn.edu

  Pocket PCIT
  Free online resource based on Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT) for children ages 2–7 with behavior challenges. Video demonstrations and guidance on building the parent-child relationship (Child-Directed Interaction) and consistent limit-setting (Parent-Directed Interaction).
  https://www.pocketpcit.com

  FAST Behavior Basics (Seattle Children's Hospital):
  On-demand, self-paced class for parents of children ages 5–12 with challenging behaviors (also relevant for ADHD, autism, anxiety, compliance difficulties). 8 core + 3 bonus skill videos (3–5 min each). Caregiver workbook available.
  Class: https://www.seattlechildrens.org/health-safety/classes-events/behavior-basics-class/
  Workbook: https://www.seattlechildrens.org/globalassets/documents/healthcare-professionals/pal/fast/fast-b-caregiver-workbook.pdf
