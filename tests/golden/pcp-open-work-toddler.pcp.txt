RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE from Developmental-Behavioral Pediatrics (DBP)

DBP follow-up:
  • We will see the child again in 3 months to complete the autism diagnostic evaluation. We will send the follow-up plan in that visit's note.

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Ask whether speech-language therapy and parent-child interaction therapy (PCIT) have started. If not, ask where the family is on each waitlist. The insurance case manager can find in-network providers, and families often have to call a waitlist more than once. We placed every referral. Some insurance plans want the referral to come from primary care; if so, please re-send it.
  • PCP and family (now): Check that the Florida Early Steps (Part C) referral was sent. Early Steps has to find the child eligible before the third birthday, so it cannot wait.

Urgent, or for another service:
  • The child loses language, social, or motor skills they already had: refer to pediatric neurology.
