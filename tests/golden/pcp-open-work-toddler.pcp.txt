RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that speech-language therapy and PCIT have started. If not, the family should ask the insurance case manager for in-network providers and keep calling the waitlists.
  • PCP and family (now): Confirm that the Florida Early Steps (Part C) referral has been submitted. Eligibility must be established before the third birthday.

DBP follow-up:
  • DBP follow-up is limited to one return visit in 3 months to complete the autism diagnostic evaluation. No routine DBP follow-up is needed after that visit, and we are always glad to see the family again if the need arises.
  • To come back: within 2 years of the last visit here, please call our clinic directly at {clinic phone number}; no new referral is needed. After 2 years, or if the child has been discharged from our clinic, please send a new referral.

Refer directly to another service if:
  • Language, social, or motor skills the child already had are lost: refer to pediatric neurology.
