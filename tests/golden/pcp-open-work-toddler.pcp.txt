RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRIMARY CARE from Developmental-Behavioral Pediatrics (DBP)

DBP follow-up:
  • DBP will see the child again in 3 months to complete the autism diagnostic evaluation. The follow-up plan after that visit will be stated in that visit's note.

Actions:
  • PCP (next visit): Confirm that speech-language therapy and parent-child interaction therapy (PCIT) have started. If not, ask where the family is on each waitlist and point them to the insurance case manager for in-network providers; waitlists often need repeated calls. Our clinic placed every referral; some insurance plans need one re-sent from primary care.
  • PCP and family (now): Confirm that the Florida Early Steps (Part C) referral has been submitted. Eligibility must be established before the third birthday.

Urgent, or for another service:
  • Language, social, or motor skills the child already had are lost: refer to pediatric neurology.
