Toe walking is considered idiopathic only when it is bilateral, present from the start of walking, persists beyond age 2 years, and has no neurologic cause; co-occurring autism or ADHD does not exclude it.¹ Toe walking is more common in autistic children and is more likely to persist without treatment than in other children.²,³
