I evaluated {Student Name} on {Date of Evaluation} and am writing to provide a formal medical recommendation in support of the family's request for special education services under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).

Specifically, I am requesting a comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation to determine eligibility for special education services. Florida allows 60 calendar days from written parental consent for that evaluation, with the exclusions the rule provides. The findings supporting that request are set out below, together with the services and accommodations I recommend.

She has received a diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) (ICD-10-CM: F84.0), classified as Level 1 (requiring support), confirmed at the evaluation referenced above.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION EVALUATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Based on the above diagnosis and identified educational impact, I strongly recommend that {Student Name} receive a comprehensive evaluation for special education eligibility under IDEA, including consideration of the Autism Spectrum Disorder eligibility category (34 C.F.R. §300.8(c)(1); Florida Rule 6A-6.03023) and any additional eligibility categories supported by the evaluation results.

For the family's planning as much as the team's, the evaluation timeline in Florida is 60 calendar days from written parental consent, excluding school holidays and breaks, summer vacation, and absences beyond eight school days (Florida Rule 6A-6.0331(3)(g)). This evaluation should include assessment of: cognitive abilities, academic achievement, adaptive behavior, communication skills, behavioral and social-emotional functioning, and sensory-motor development.

A comprehensive psychoeducational evaluation is recommended to characterize {Student Name}'s academic achievement and cognitive processing profile.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED SERVICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If eligible for special education services, I recommend the IEP include the following:

Psychoeducational Evaluation: A comprehensive psychoeducational evaluation is recommended to fully characterize the academic achievement and cognitive processing profile. The evaluation should include: cognitive assessment (IQ and index scores), academic achievement testing across reading, mathematics, and written expression (WIAT-4 or equivalent), phonological processing assessment (CTOPP-2 if reading concerns are present), working memory and processing speed indices, and a Specific Learning Disability (SLD) eligibility determination per Florida Rule 6A-6.03018. Results will directly inform IEP goal development and eligibility determination.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED ACCOMMODATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I recommend that the IEP team consider the following accommodations, calibrated to evaluation findings and individual educational need. These are clinical recommendations, not legal entitlements; the team's selection should reflect what the evaluation supports.

Core ASD-Related Accommodations:
The following accommodations have broad evidence support across the autism spectrum. Implementation should be calibrated to this student's developmental level and individual presentation.
  • Predictable daily schedule with advance written or visual notice before transitions (schedule format and complexity calibrated to developmental level)
  • Visual supports (format calibrated to developmental level and literacy): pictorial schedule and visual transition cues for younger or non-reading students; written agenda, task checklists, or digital planner for older or verbal students — visual representation of schedules, task steps, and expectations reduces transition anxiety and supports regulation across the autism spectrum
