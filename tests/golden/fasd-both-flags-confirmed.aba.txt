LETTER OF MEDICAL NECESSITY
Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) Therapy — Autism Spectrum Disorder

Date: [DATE]
Diagnosis: Autism Spectrum Disorder, Level 1 — Requiring Support (DSM-5)
ICD-10-CM: F84.0

To Whom It May Concern / Dear Medical Director:

I write to request authorization for Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy for the patient, who has received a confirmed diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Level 1 — Requiring Support, as defined by the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition (DSM-5; American Psychiatric Association, 2013) enrolled under commercial/private insurance. ABA therapy is medically necessary, as documented below.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
I. DIAGNOSIS & LEVEL OF SUPPORT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Without support in place, deficits in social communication cause noticeable impairments in his functioning. He experiences difficulty initiating social interactions and demonstrates atypical or unsuccessful responses to social overtures from others. Behavioral inflexibility causes difficulties in at least one context. Individualized ABA therapy is medically necessary to establish functional communication, social, and adaptive skills and to build behavioral repertoires that reduce the need for caregiver-mediated support over time.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
II. FUNCTIONAL IMPAIRMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
He demonstrates clinically significant functional impairments across the following domains: expressive and/or receptive communication; behavioral regulation and reduction of interfering behaviors; social interaction and peer relationships. With respect to communication, he uses simple sentences.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
III. TREATMENT NECESSITY & EVIDENCE BASE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ABA therapy, the most extensively studied behavioral intervention for ASD, is explicitly recommended as a primary evidence-based treatment by the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP).¹ Multiple federal agency systematic reviews confirm its efficacy across communication, adaptive behavior, and quality-of-life outcomes.²

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IV. REQUESTED TREATMENT PARAMETERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Requested intensity: 20 hours per week
Priority treatment targets:
  • comm
  • Imitation and observational learning
  • Play skills and peer interaction
  • Transition tolerance and behavioral flexibility

Treatment will be implemented by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) who will oversee program development, conduct ongoing assessment, and adjust treatment based on objective data. Direct therapy will be provided by trained Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) under BCBA supervision, consistent with BACB professional and ethical standards.
