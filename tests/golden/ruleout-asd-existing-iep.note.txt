Programming should be individualized, age-appropriate, and promote self-determination and skill generalization across natural environments.
  • Parent/caregiver training: Caregiver-implemented intervention is a required and evidence-based component of ABA services; specific training content is determined by the treating BCBA and commonly spans the function of target behaviors, reinforcement strategies in natural routines, generalization of clinic and school skills to the home, and crisis response for high-intensity behaviors.
